LIBRARY RULES — TA2 RAG POLICY DOCUMENT

1. Loan periods
- Novel: 14 days.
- Textbook: 30 days.
- Reference: 7 days and reference books are for library use unless a librarian grants an exception.

2. Fine slabs
- Novel: ₹2 per overdue day.
- Textbook: ₹5 per overdue day.
- Reference: ₹10 per overdue day.

3. Fine calculation
The fine is the applicable daily rate multiplied by the number of overdue days. The calculator tool performs the arithmetic; policy retrieval supplies the applicable rule.

4. Fine waiver
A waiver may be requested for exceptional circumstances. A waiver must not be granted automatically by an AI agent. Human approval from a librarian is required before a waiver is confirmed.

5. Policy coverage
The policy document covers loan periods, supported book types, daily fine slabs, and waiver approval. If a requested policy rule is not present in this document, the assistant must say that the policy chunk was not found and must not invent a rule.
